# ✈️ British Airways Passenger Satisfaction Dashboard (Tableau)

## Project Overview
This project presents an **interactive Tableau dashboard** analyzing **passenger satisfaction for British Airways** across multiple dimensions, including cabin staff service, entertainment quality, food & beverages, ground service, seat comfort, and value for money.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to explore satisfaction trends **across continents, aircraft types, seat classes, traveler types, and time**, helping identify service strengths, uncover gaps, and support data-driven improvements to the overall travel experience.

## Tools Used
- Tableau  
  - Interactive dashboard design  
  - Calculated fields and KPI metrics  
  - Filters and parameter-driven analysis  
  - Geographic (map-based) visualizations  
- Customer experience & service analytics concepts

## Dataset Description
- **Type:** Airline passenger review and satisfaction data  
- **Scope:** Global passenger feedback across multiple years  
- **Key attributes include:**
  - Overall rating
  - Cabin staff service rating
  - Entertainment rating
  - Food & beverage rating
  - Ground service rating
  - Seat comfort rating
  - Value for money
  - Continent and country
  - Aircraft type
  - Seat class
  - Traveler type
  - Review date

## Dashboard Components & Analysis

### Key Satisfaction KPIs
- Overall average rating
- Average cabin staff service score
- Average ratings for:
  - Entertainment
  - Ground service
  - Seat comfort
  - Value for money
  - Food & beverages

These KPIs provide a **high-level view of passenger sentiment**.

### Satisfaction by Country & Continent
- World map showing **average cabin staff service ratings by country**
- Enables regional comparison and identification of high- and low-performing markets
- Supports region-specific service improvement strategies

### Satisfaction Trends Over Time
- Line chart tracking average ratings by month/year
- Highlights changes in passenger sentiment over time
- Useful for identifying:
  - Service improvements
  - Operational disruptions
  - External impact periods
    
### Satisfaction by Aircraft Type
- Comparison of average service ratings across different aircraft groups
- Helps evaluate consistency of service quality across fleet types

### Interactive Segmentation
Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:
- Continent
- Metric (overall rating, cabin staff service, entertainment, food, etc.)
- Seat type (Economy, Premium Economy, Business, First)
- Traveler type (Business, Leisure, Family, Solo)
- Time period

This allows **granular exploration** of passenger experience across multiple dimensions.

## Key Insights
- Cabin staff service receives relatively higher ratings compared to other service dimensions
- Passenger satisfaction varies notably across regions and aircraft types
- Certain service categories, such as entertainment and food, show lower average ratings
- Satisfaction trends fluctuate over time, indicating opportunities for continuous monitoring and improvement

## Business Value
This dashboard demonstrates how customer experience analytics can:
- Identify strengths and weaknesses in airline service delivery
- Support targeted service improvements by region or fleet
- Enhance passenger satisfaction and brand perception
- Enable data-driven decision-making for airline operations and customer experience teams
